Common question

Why use BriefAI instead of a general AI chat?

A general AI chat can also explain a letter and draft a reply if you provide the text and suitable instructions. BriefAI guides you through a workflow for German letters: choose a photo or PDF, recognize text on your device, read a structured explanation in your chosen language, check possible dates and actions against the original, and edit a German reply draft yourself. This is about convenience and review, not a promise of greater legal accuracy. AI can make mistakes; seek qualified advice when the stakes are high.
