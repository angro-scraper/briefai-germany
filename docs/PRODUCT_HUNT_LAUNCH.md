# BriefAI Germany — Product Hunt launch pack

## Launch gate

Do **not** submit before all of these are true on a real device:

- email, Google and Apple sign-in complete without an error;
- the first analysis completes and the result can be read;
- the five-free-analysis limit leads to a working, clearly priced purchase
  choice; and
- the App Store and Google Play links lead to the public listings.

Product Hunt requires a personal maker account. The maker must publish as a
real person, not as an AMIA or BriefAI brand account. The launch is free. Do
not ask anyone to upvote or offer anything in exchange for an upvote; invite
feedback and conversation instead.

Official guidance:
<https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17350772-launch-guide>

### Short description

> BriefAI turns German official letters into a clear checklist: what the
> letter says, the deadline, required documents, and a German reply draft to
> review. Start with five free analyses. It is an information tool, not legal
> advice.

### Maker comment

> Hi Product Hunt — we built BriefAI Germany for the moment when an official
> German letter arrives and the language makes the next step unclear. The app
> helps people structure the sender, deadline, requested documents and a
> possible formal reply draft in their chosen language. It does not replace a
> lawyer or an adviser; users must review the original letter and important
> decisions themselves. We would genuinely value feedback on clarity, language
> support and the first-analysis experience.

## Gallery plan

1. **Letter → clarity:** a fictional Jobcenter-style letter beside a plain
   language summary, deadline and next-step checklist.
2. **Language choice:** the language selector plus an explanation in a
   selected language. Never use a real customer document.
3. **Reply review:** an editable German response draft, clearly labeled for
   user review before it is sent.

The first image must contain the product name and a readable one-line promise.
Use only actual UI from the current public build; do not create a mockup for a
feature that is unavailable.

## Launch-day conduct

- Link to the product page and both app stores.
- Respond to questions as the maker/team with the legal-information disclaimer
  when someone asks for individual legal advice.
- Ask for feedback, never votes, reviews or installs.
- Record Product Hunt traffic under `utm_source=producthunt`; compare it with
  consented landing clicks, authenticated registrations and verified purchases.
