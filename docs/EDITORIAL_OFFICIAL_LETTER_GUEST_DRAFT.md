# A German official letter arrived: five checks before you reply

**Proposed byline:** AMIA Technologies, the team behind BriefAI Germany

An official-looking letter can feel urgent, especially if German is not your
first language. The useful first step is not to write a response immediately.
It is to make a small, accurate record of what the document actually says.
That helps you ask a clearer question of the authority or an adviser and
reduces the risk of overlooking a date or requested document.

The following checklist is general information. It cannot tell you whether a
particular decision is lawful, which remedy applies, or when your individual
deadline expires. Those questions depend on the original document and your
circumstances.

## 1. Confirm who sent it

Find the authority's name, postal address and the office dealing with your
case. A *Jobcenter*, *Familienkasse*, *Ausländerbehörde* and *Finanzamt* do not
handle the same matters. If a link, phone number, email address or payment
request looks unusual, check it against the authority's independently found
official website before entering personal information or making a payment.
The Federal Employment Agency has warned about people impersonating it or
Jobcenters to obtain sensitive data. A familiar logo alone is not proof of
authenticity. [Source: Bundesagentur für Arbeit warning about fake contacts](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/news/fake-anrufe).

## 2. Copy the reference and identify the type of letter

Write down the *Aktenzeichen*, *Kundennummer* or other reference exactly as it
appears. Then look for the document's label and its operative sentence: is it
a decision (*Bescheid*), a request for evidence (*Nachweise*), a hearing
(*Anhörung*), or an appointment (*Termin*)? These words are clues, not a
complete legal classification. For example, the Federal Employment Agency
explains that an unemployment-benefit *Bescheid* can communicate an award,
change or repayment decision. [Source: Bundesagentur für Arbeit on unemployment-benefit decisions](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/das-muessen-sie-beachten/arbeitslosengeld-bescheid).

If the letter has attachments or several pages, read the complete document.
The action requested may be in a later paragraph while the relevant
instructions or rights appear at the end.

## 3. Separate every date

Make four separate notes: the date printed on the letter, the date you
received it, any appointment date, and every date or period stated for a
response, payment, evidence or remedy. Keep the envelope or delivery record
with the original. Do not calculate a legal deadline from the letter's date
alone: the rules can depend on the procedure, delivery and wording of the
notice. If the period might be short, contact the authority or a qualified
advice service promptly rather than waiting for a translation or an app to
make the decision for you.

Look for *Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung* near the end of a decision. It describes the
available route for challenging that decision. The Federal Employment Agency
explicitly notes that asking it to explain a decision does not replace a
formal objection. [Source: Bundesagentur für Arbeit on problems with a decision](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/das-muessen-sie-beachten/arbeitslosengeld-bescheid).

## 4. Turn the request into a short checklist

Underline each verb that tells you to do something: send, appear, pay,
confirm, explain or contact. Beside each one, note the exact document,
information, amount or period mentioned. Check whether the letter specifies
an official portal, form or delivery method. A general feedback form may not
be the correct channel for a formal remedy; the Federal Employment Agency
warns that feedback does not replace an objection or court action.
[Source: Bundesagentur für Arbeit feedback guidance](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/ueber-uns/anregungen-kritik-und-lob).

Keep a copy of anything you submit and a record of when and how you sent it.
If you cannot provide an item, ask the office what to do rather than silently
ignoring the request. Do not send more personal documents than the authority
asks for without considering whether they are necessary.

## 5. Decide what needs a human answer

A translation or plain-language summary can help you understand the vocabulary
and prepare a question. It cannot verify that a decision is correct or choose
a legal strategy. Ask the authority to clarify unclear wording; for an appeal,
threatened loss of benefits, immigration consequence, debt collection or
other high-stakes matter, seek qualified individual advice early. The German
federal service portal describes an objection as a way to challenge an
administrative decision, but the details vary by region and procedure.
[Source: Bundesportal on objections to administrative decisions](https://verwaltung.bund.de/leistungsverzeichnis/DE/leistung/99146009029000).

Before replying, you should be able to say in one sentence: “This office,
under reference number X, asks me to do Y by the date shown in the letter.”
If you cannot say that confidently, clarification is the next step. A short,
accurate question is more useful than a polished reply to the wrong issue.
